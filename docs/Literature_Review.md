Literature Review
The literature review for this project focuses on understanding existing digital loan applications and loan management platforms. 

Since we are developing a Loan Lifecycle Management System for Micro-Loans, it is important to study how current applications handle loan application, user registration, eligibility checking, repayment tracking, payment options, notifications, privacy, and security. Each team member should individually study at least two loan applications, making a total of eight applications, and analyze their strengths, drawbacks, and possible improvements.

The purpose of this literature review is not only to list existing applications, but also to understand their design, performance, data accuracy, compatibility, security, privacy, and future improvement scope. This analysis will help us identify useful features for our system and also understand the gaps that our project can address.

1. KreditBee

KreditBee is a digital lending platform that provides personal loans through an online process. The platform allows users to apply for loans digitally by entering personal details, completing KYC, checking eligibility, selecting loan amount and tenure, and repaying the loan through digital payment methods.

In terms of user registration, KreditBee uses mobile number verification, profile creation, and KYC-based onboarding. Users are required to provide personal details such as name, phone number, email address, address, date of birth, and identity-related information. This makes the registration process structured and useful for borrower verification.

For loan application, KreditBee provides a simple online process where users can choose the loan amount and repayment tenure based on their eligibility. This reduces paperwork and makes the borrowing process faster compared to traditional loan methods. The application flow is suitable for users who need quick personal loans or small-value credit.

For eligibility checking, KreditBee considers borrower details, credit profile, repayment capacity, and other internal criteria. However, the exact risk evaluation logic is not visible to users. A borrower may know whether they are eligible or not, but the system does not clearly explain how the eligibility decision is calculated.

For repayment tracking, KreditBee supports EMI-based repayment and allows users to view repayment details. It also provides digital payment support, including UPI-related payment services. This makes repayment easier and more convenient for borrowers.
For notifications, KreditBee may use app alerts, SMS, or other communication methods to update users about loan status, repayment reminders, and payment-related information. This helps users stay informed about due dates and repayment activity.

In terms of privacy and security, KreditBee collects sensitive user data such as personal information, location, device information, KYC details, and financial data. Its privacy policy mentions that data may be used for identity verification, KYC, fraud prevention, customer support, transaction history, marketing, and service improvement. This shows that privacy and secure data handling are important in digital lending systems.

Advantages Of KreditBee
1. The registration and loan application process is mostly digital and user-friendly.
2. It reduces paperwork and allows users to apply for loans online.
3. It provides quick eligibility checking and loan processing for eligible users.
4. It supports flexible loan amount and repayment tenure selection.
5. It provides digital repayment options, including UPI-based payments.
6. It gives a good example of borrower-side loan application design.
7. KYC and borrower data collection make the verification process structured.

Drawbacks Of KreditBee
1. The internal eligibility and risk evaluation logic is not transparent to users.
2. The admin-side loan approval workflow is not visible.
3. Users may not clearly understand processing fees, interest, penalty charges, and late payment conditions unless they read all terms carefully.
4. Since it collects sensitive personal, location, device, and financial data, privacy concerns may arise.
5. The system is commercial and closed, so it cannot be customized for academic or small-scale institutional use.
6. It focuses mainly on fast loan processing, while our project needs to clearly show the complete lifecycle including admin review, repayment tracking, and default monitoring.

Future Scope Based On KreditBee Analysis
1. Eligibility decisions can be made more transparent by showing simple reasons for approval or rejection.
2. Charges, interest, and late payment rules can be explained more clearly.
3. Borrowers can be given better visibility into repayment history and default risk.
4. More user control can be provided over personal data and consent.
5. A clearer admin-side dashboard can be designed for application review, repayment tracking, and default monitoring.


2. CASHe
CASHe is a digital lending platform that mainly provides personal loans to salaried users. It focuses on fast, paperless loan application and digital repayment. CASHe is useful for studying how employment and income details can be used for eligibility checking in loan applications.

In terms of user registration, CASHe requires users to create an account and provide personal, employment, and KYC-related details. Since the platform mainly targets salaried users, employment information and repayment capacity are important parts of the onboarding process.

For loan application, CASHe allows users to apply digitally by submitting required details and documents. The application process is paperless and designed to reduce manual effort. This is useful for understanding how loan application forms can collect user profile, income, and identity-related data.

For eligibility checking, CASHe uses internal credit rating and verification criteria. A user’s application can be rejected if documents are not uploaded, false information is provided, the user does not have a job or repayment capacity, or the user profile does not match the required criteria. This shows that eligibility checking depends on document verification, employment status, repayment capacity, and internal scoring.

For repayment tracking, CASHe supports digital repayment and provides loan-related details to users. Borrowers can manage repayment through the app or platform, which helps reduce manual payment tracking.
For payment options, CASHe supports digital repayment methods. These methods are useful because they allow borrowers to make payments online instead of visiting a physical branch.

For notifications, CASHe can communicate application status, repayment reminders, and account-related updates through digital channels such as app notifications, SMS, or email. This helps users stay updated on loan activities.
In terms of privacy and security, CASHe’s privacy policy highlights the importance of user privacy and data security. It mentions consent-based access to data, KYC-related permissions, data retention for regulatory compliance, session data clearing, and restrictions on unnecessary access to phone resources such as files, contacts, call logs, and telephony functions. It also states that data is stored on servers located in India, which is important from a regulatory and privacy perspective.

Advantages Of CASHe
1. The loan application process is paperless and convenient.
2. It is useful for salaried users who need quick personal loans.
3. It considers employment details, documents, and repayment capacity for eligibility.
4. It supports digital repayment, reducing the need for manual payment handling.
5. It provides a good example of profile-based and income-based loan evaluation.
6. Privacy policy mentions consent, data security, and limited access to mobile resources.
7. It supports digital communication for loan status and repayment-related updates.

Drawbacks Of CASHe
1. The exact internal credit rating and eligibility logic are not fully visible to users.
2. It is mainly suitable for salaried users, so it may not support all borrower categories.
3. The admin-side loan approval, repayment monitoring, and default tracking workflow is not visible.
4. Users may not clearly understand why their application is rejected.
5. Loan charges, penalties, and repayment conditions may require careful reading.
6. Since sensitive personal, employment, KYC, and financial data are collected, privacy and data protection remain important concerns.

Future Scope Based On CASHe Analysis
1. The application can provide clearer reasons for approval or rejection.
2. Eligibility criteria can be explained in a more user-friendly way.
3. More borrower categories can be supported beyond salaried users.
4. Repayment schedules, overdue charges, and default conditions can be shown more clearly.
5. User privacy controls can be improved by making data consent and data usage easier to understand.
6. Admin-side monitoring features can be represented more clearly in academic systems like ours.

Conclusion
From the study of KreditBee and CASHe, we understood that existing digital lending applications provide fast online loan application, user registration, KYC verification, eligibility checking, digital repayment, and notifications. However, both platforms have limitations in transparency. Their internal risk evaluation, approval workflow, admin-side monitoring, and default tracking are not clearly visible to users.
This gives direction for our proposed Loan Lifecycle Management System. Our system should include user registration, loan application, rule-based eligibility checking, repayment tracking, payment support, notifications, privacy controls, and security features. At the same time, it should clearly show the admin workflow, risk evaluation result, repayment history, overdue cases, and default monitoring so that the complete loan lifecycle is understandable and manageable.
